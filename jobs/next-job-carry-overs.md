# Carry-overs for the job after job 10

Found after job 10 was paid (2026-10-22, 15:37 UTC), so they ride at the top of the next job (phase 3 or a job 12), before its own work. Full wording here; the request names each in one line.

(h) Phantom scroll gap under the findings (SPEC 3.2, decision 37). On the live Builder at 1920×1080 the sticky right column (.hookcol, overflow auto) scrolls 243 px past the bottom of the findings box, an empty strip with its own scrollbar; about 350 px at 1366×768; the amount varies with how much of the card's details area and the findings list is clipped. Cause: the visually hidden labels (.sr, position absolute) inside .hc-scroll and .findings .list, two scroll containers that have no position, take the sticky column as their containing block and sit at their unclipped static positions below the visible area, which extends the column's scrollable overflow. Fix: give every scroll container position: relative (.hc-scroll, .findings .list, .understood, .shelf, .marquee, and any other element with overflow auto or scroll), verified on the live page by injecting that rule (scrollHeight 1568 to 1325 at 1920×1080, the content plus 6 px padding). Tests: a unit test that every selector with overflow auto or scroll in styles.css also sets position; a Playwright test at 1920×1080 and 1366×768 with seven findings showing that asserts the column's scrollHeight equals its last child's bottom plus the padding, within 2 px, both at load and after the column is scrolled to its end.

(i) The Builder goes fluid (SPEC 3.2 and decision 38, replacing the 1240 px cap). .builder-page.wrap takes max-width 1800px with padding-inline clamp(16px, 3vw, 48px) (keeping the safe-area max() on each side); the .b2 grid keeps steps minmax(0, 1fr) and the card becomes clamp(420px, 28vw, 520px) from 1100 px up; the sticky column, the shelf, the rule rows and the fields fill their column; paragraph text keeps its 65ch measure; type sizes stay fixed. The width sweep adds 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 and asserts that the content's left and right edges sit within the gutter of the window edges up to 1800 px (at 2560 the content is 1800 px wide and centred, the card 520 px), that nothing scrolls sideways, and that the card still fits the viewport. The rest of the site keeps its 1180 px reading width.
