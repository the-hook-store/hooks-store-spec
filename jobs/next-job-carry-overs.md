# Carry-overs for a later job 14

Job 13 was paid on 2026-10-22 at 20:07 UTC and accepted at 21:10 UTC (decision 48). Nothing further is planned; these ride at the top of a job 14 if one is ever staged.

(r) Live without IMD's record plane (decision 48). A fresh browser resolves Live through the relay's /intake and /jobs reads; when IMD answers 503 "busy" or 502, every hook reads "In progress" and the strip loses its placements, as happened on 2026-10-22 around 21:00 UTC on both the job 12 and job 13 builds. Fix: the spec repository's scheduled script (scripts/proven_index.py, every six hours) also writes evidence/launch-records.json, a snapshot per store launch of id, launch number, chain, status, request id, artifacts with addresses, and the seeded pool key; the site fetches it at load like the proven index and uses it to resolve addresses and Live whenever /intake or /jobs fail or time out, confirming the hook's code on chain as today; the chain scan keeps working for new requests. Playwright: with /jobs and /intake answering 503, the catalog shows the four live hooks as Live from the snapshot.

(s) Log windows and backoff (SPEC 7.2, decision 48). The publicnode endpoints refuse the site's eth_getLogs windows with 403 (ranges above their cap) and eth.llamarpc.com answered 525, so the fallback never helps a scan and a rate-limited primary turns into a storm of failing calls (81 failed Robinhood reads in one page load). Fix: a per-endpoint maximum log range in site-config.json (publicnode: 10,000 blocks), windows split to fit the endpoint in use, a backoff of 2, 4, 8 seconds on 429 before any retry, at most two log scans in flight per chain, and the validation run includes a scan against the real endpoints with the counts of failed calls in the results (must be zero on a quiet network).
