Carry-overs from job 11 (Builder v2, delivered 2026-10-22 at commit e1112f9b, checked on the live site), before everything else:

(a) The hook card's scrolling details area (.hc-scroll, holding the tiles, the money line and the readiness list) is sized by a layout effect from the card's document position (getBoundingClientRect().top + scrollY), so once the sticky column is stuck and anything re-renders (a keystroke in a field, a poll) the area collapses to the 140 px floor and stays there until a re-render back at the top of the page: at 1440×900 it goes from 182 px to 140 px, at 2560×1300 from 582 px to 140 px, hiding most of the hook. Size it from the card's on-screen position instead (the top of its bounding rectangle in the viewport, which equals the sticky offset once stuck), recompute on scroll (through requestAnimationFrame) and on resize, and never let it shrink below its size at load while the column is stuck. A Playwright test at 1440×900 and at 2560×1300 scrolls down 1000 px, types in Owner address, and asserts .hc-scroll is at least as tall as at load and at least 300 px, and that the card still fits the viewport.
